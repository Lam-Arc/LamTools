# Synthesize

Produce one decision-ready view without breaking provenance. Integration is not permission to erase disagreement.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the question, audience, output contract, schema, granularity, and decision the result should support.
2. Inventory the available evidence package records. Reject summary-only delegated findings unless their evidence artifact is accessible.
3. Normalize entities, dates, units, and terminology while preserving original values, transformations, and assumptions in `TRACE_MAP_CONTRACT.md`.
4. Group source facts, computed facts, inferences, and predictions separately. Link each consequential statement to supporting and contradicting evidence.
5. Resolve explainable conflicts by scope, time, or method; present unresolved conflicts and coverage gaps instead of smoothing them away.
6. Lead with the answer, then give the minimum supporting structure: evidence, calculations, confidence reasons, limitations, and missing information.

Do not fill missing fields with plausible prose. Use null, unknown, a range, or an explicit estimate with assumptions as the output contract allows.
